## Scope

`BrassCompassItem`: registered, stack size one, `inventoryTick` sets vanilla's `lodestone_tracker` from the chosen entry of the holder's dimension (`COMPASS-REQ-005`, `-006`, `-015`), lost and revived marking on tick (`REQ-008`, `-009`), tooltip with the destination (`REQ-012`). Item model: a copy of vanilla's `items/compass.json` over 32 frames whose textures are brass-tinted copies of vanilla's, generated by `tools/needle.py` from the Minecraft jar in the Gradle cache and committed.

## Approach

An `Item` subclass (not `CompassItem`) so `use` and `useOn` are ours; `inventoryTick` derives the tracker each server tick; `tools/needle.py` extracts vanilla's 32 compass frames from the Minecraft jar in the Gradle cache, tints them brass and writes the frame models and the item model.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Game test: an entry chosen in the overworld sets the tracker to it; none chosen leaves it untracked; breaking the lodestone marks the entry lost within a tick and placing it back revives it (`NeedleGameTest`).
- [x] The 32 frames and the item model exist and reference each other; `just client` shows a brass needle (Kevin's check). `just needle` wrote 32 tinted textures, 32 frame models and the item model from vanilla's; the in-game look is Kevin's check at the screens ticket.
- [x] Tooltip shows the chosen entry's name or 'no destination' (`appendHoverText`, translation keys in `en_us`).
